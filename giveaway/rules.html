Vervotech LinkedIn Travel Voucher Giveaway: Official Rules

Promoter

Vervotech Solutions ("Vervotech"). Contact: via a message to the Vervotech LinkedIn page.

Giveaway period

Starts 23 October 2026 and closes 11 November 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Entries after the closing time will not count.

Who can enter

  • Residents of India aged 18 or over with a personal LinkedIn account.
  • Employees of Vervotech, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.

How to enter

  1. Follow the Vervotech company page on LinkedIn.
  2. Comment on the post featured at the top of the Vervotech LinkedIn page. Any genuine comment counts.

One entry per person, however many comments you post. No purchase is necessary. Following and commenting are free. Automated, bulk or fake-account entries will be disqualified.

Prize

One (1) travel gift voucher worth ₹10,000, from a travel booking brand chosen by Vervotech. The prize is non-transferable, has no cash alternative and is subject to the voucher brand's terms and expiry.

Winner selection

One winner will be drawn at random from all eligible entries on 12 November 2026. The winner must still be following the Vervotech page at the time of the draw. The winner will be announced in a reply to their comment and contacted by LinkedIn message. If the winner does not reply within 7 days, Vervotech may draw a new winner.

Your data

Vervotech uses your LinkedIn name and comment only to run this giveaway and contact the winner. We will not sell your data.

  • Vervotech may cancel or change the giveaway if it can no longer be run fairly, and will post any change on its LinkedIn page.
  • Vervotech's decision on the winner is final.
